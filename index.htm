About Me

My technical skills include SolidWorks, ANSYS, CATIA, AutoCAD, and M.S. Office. My curriculum expertise is in 3D Designing and Modelling, and Simulation using Fluent & Static Structural. I have a keen interest in Simulations, 3D Modelling, Sheet Metal, HVAC, and Heat Exchangers.
